KAF81 Scholarships 2025-26

KAF81 is committed to support more students each year.

Online application for KAF81 Scholarships 2025-26 is now CLOSED

Eligibility Criteria

KAF81 Scholarships are available for:

  • Students of B-Tech and IDD at IIT-BHU (all years)
  • Students who are economically weak or have high academic performance
  • Regular, admitted students with an Institute-provided Roll Number
  • Students not receiving any other scholarships

Application Process

The scholarship application process involves:

  1. Online application submission
  2. Screening by a committee of batchmates
  3. Shortlisting of candidates meeting merit and means criteria
  4. Personal interviews with panels of alumni from the class of 1981
  5. Recommendations to the Foundation's board for disbursal
